# Pirate Ship Escape Room

## Overview

Pirate Ship Escape Room is an interactive escape room experience set aboard a pirate ship. Players explore different areas of the ship, solve puzzles, discover hidden clues, and collect keys to unlock new rooms and progress through the game.

## Levels

* **Ship Deck** – The starting area where players begin their escape room adventure.
* **Storage Room** – Explore the storage area, discover clues, and solve puzzles to progress.
* **Captain Cabin** – Investigate the captain's quarters and uncover hidden secrets.
* **Navigation Room** – Solve the final challenges and complete the escape room.

## Gameplay

* Explore an interactive pirate ship environment.
* Find hidden clues and solve puzzles.
* Interact with objects to uncover secrets.
* Collect keys to unlock doors and access new levels.
* Progress through the ship by completing challenges.
